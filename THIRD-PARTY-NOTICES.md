## Tools used at run time, not distributed

pasc runs these programs when a command needs them. They are installed separately, and nothing of them is included in pasc.

| tool | used by | license |
|---|---|---|
| [Free Pascal](https://www.freepascal.org) compiler | `build`, `test`, through lazbuild | GPL-2.0 |
| [Lazarus](https://www.lazarus-ide.org) `lazbuild` | `build`, `test`, `watch` | GPL-2.0 (IDE and tools) |
| [boss](https://github.com/HashLoad/boss) | `new`, `deps` | MIT |
| [git](https://git-scm.com) | `new`, `add` | GPL-2.0 |
| [PasDoc](https://github.com/pasdoc/pasdoc) | `docs` | GPL-2.0 |
| [kcov](https://github.com/SimonKagstrom/kcov) | `coverage` | GPL-2.0 |
