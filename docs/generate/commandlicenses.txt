@title(Licenses)
@shorttitle(Licenses)

@section(1 SecFirst Description)

Shows the pasc license (MIT) and the notices of the third-party software, the same text as the LICENSE and THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md files of the repository, which are embedded in the executable. So the notices go with pasc even when the executable is copied without the release zip.

The notices list:

@unorderedList(
  @item(the software included in the executable: pascli, and the Free Pascal and Lazarus units it is compiled with;)
  @item(the resources embedded in it, like the template of the .gitignore;)
  @item(the tools pasc runs but does not distribute: Free Pascal, lazbuild, boss, git and PasDoc.)
)

They also say that the files pasc generates in your projects, like the templates of @link(commandnew pasc new --template), can be used with no restriction and no attribution.

Example:
@longCode(
  pasc licenses
)
